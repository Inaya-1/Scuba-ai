# Demo Video Assets

Place MP4 dive footage files here for Demo Mode:

- `reef-dive.mp4` — Coral reef POV footage
- `wreck-dive.mp4` — Wreck exploration footage  
- `night-dive.mp4` — Night dive footage

## Getting demo footage

You can download royalty-free scuba footage from:
- [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/search/videos/scuba%20diving/)
- [Pixabay](https://pixabay.com/videos/search/scuba%20diving/)

Or use the "Custom URL" option in Demo Mode to paste a direct link to any MP4 video.

## Note on YouTube
YouTube videos cannot be directly loaded due to cross-origin restrictions.
For the "Wizard of Oz" demo, point the phone's camera at a monitor playing YouTube instead,
or download the video as MP4 and place it here.
